 Existence
* Does this exist?
* What exists?
* Does it still exist?
* Did it ever exist?

 Identity
* What is it?
* Who is it?
* Is it the same thing as before?
* What distinguishes it from everything else?

 Knowledge
* What do I know?
* What don't I know?
* What do I know that I cannot access?
* What do I suspect?
* What do I merely believe?
* What have I forgotten?

 Provenance
* How do I know this?
* Where did this come from?
* Who told me?
* Which sensor observed it?
* Which document supports it?
* Can the source be verified?

 Confidence
* How certain am I?
* Why am I that certain?
* Has confidence increased?
* Has confidence decreased?
* What evidence would change my mind?

 Time
* When was this observed?
* When was it last confirmed?
* Has it expired?
* Is it historical?
* Is it current?
* Is it predicted?

 Witness
* Who witnessed this?
* Did I witness it directly?
* Is it secondhand?
* Is it hearsay?
* Is it inferred?

 Causality
* What caused this?
* What did this cause?
* What depends on it?
* What changed because of it?
* What would happen if it were false?

 Decisions
* What decision was made?
* Why was it made?
* What alternatives existed?
* Who made it?
* Can it be reversed?

 Intent
* What am I trying to accomplish?
* What goal does this support?
* Is it still relevant?
* Has the goal changed?

 Promises
* What have I promised?
* What has been promised to me?
* Is the promise fulfilled?
* Has the promise been broken?
* Is it still possible to fulfill?

 Behavior
* What habit resulted?
* What rule resulted?
* What policy resulted?
* What permanently changed?

 Relationships
* What is connected to this?
* What contains it?
* What does it contain?
* What resembles it?
* What contradicts it?

 Contradictions
* What conflicts with this?
* Which version is newer?
* Are both true under different conditions?
* Is this unresolved?

 Permissions
* May I reveal this?
* May I modify it?
* May I delete it?
* Who owns it?
* Who may access it?

 Unknowable
* What exists beyond my access?
* Why can't I access it?
* Is it encrypted?
* Is it forgotten?
* Is permission required?
* Can it ever become knowable?

 Forgetting
* Should this be forgotten?
* Why was it forgotten?
* Is it archived?
* Is it obsolete?
* Is only the lesson retained?

 Reflection
* What have I learned?
* What patterns have emerged?
* What mistakes keep repeating?
* What surprised me?
* What would I do differently?

 Value
* Why does this matter?
* Who benefits?
* What is its cost?
* What is its importance?
* Can it be ignored?

 Action
* What should I do?
* What can I do?
* What must I never do?
* What should happen next?

 Meta
* Why do I remember this?
* Why don't I remember that?
* Which memory system produced this?
* Can this memory be trusted?
* Should this memory be promoted?
* Should this memory become a scar?

i'd probably try to organize them into "books" that together define an intelligent agent:

1. *Ontology* — What exists?
2. *Epistemology* — What do I know, and why?
3. *Chronology* — When did it happen?
4. *Agency* — What am I trying to do?
5. *Transformation* — What changed me?
6. *Stewardship* — What am I allowed to do?
7. *Reflection* — What have I learned?
